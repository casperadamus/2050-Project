# CSE2050-Group-Project
CSE-2050-SEC012L-015L-1263

Group Project
The group project is designed to help you connect course concepts to real problem-solving, build confidence working on a larger program, and strengthen teamwork and communication skills. This semester, you will complete a scaffolded project that demonstrates how choosing different and more efficient data structures can improve program design and performance.

Teams and Milestones
You will work in teams of two and submit one shared submission per milestone. The project includes three milestones that build on course modules and give you structured practice applying what you learn:

Milestone 1: due Wednesday, March 4
Milestone 2: due Wednesday, April 8
Milestone 3: due Wednesday, April 29
Team Formation
By the end of Week 2, you will have the opportunity to submit a team request form. To form a pair, both students must be enrolled in the same lab section and must submit matching requests. If you do not have a partner, do not submit the form. We will match you with another student.

Support and Collaboration
Beginning in Lab 3, you will have dedicated time to work with your partner, ask questions, and get help from the TA. After each milestone, you will complete a brief peer evaluation to support fair grading.

You are responsible for scheduling time outside of class to collaborate and for communicating professionally with your partner. If teamwork issues arise, please reach out early to your TA and instructor. Being proactive helps us resolve issues quickly and effectively.

You may discuss the group milestones with other students on our Discord server, but please keep discussions conceptual and do not post actual code. Small snippets from related problems to help convey an idea are fine.
