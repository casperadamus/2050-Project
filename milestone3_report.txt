Put: O(1) average, O(n) Worst, w/rehash O(n)
Get: O(1) average, O(n) Worst
Merge: O(nlogn)
quick: O(nlogn), O(n^2) worst

Merge sort: Sorted lists, guarantee, stability
Quick sort: Memory, in place sorting
The O(n^2) can be fixed by choosing a random pivot.
